About EmbroideryConvert

Free embroidery file converter — 47+ formats supported

👋 Our Mission

We built EmbroideryConvert to solve real problems that embroiderers face every day. Whether it's converting embroidery files between incompatible machine formats, or matching thread colors across DMC, Anchor, Cosmo, and J&P Coats — our tools exist to save you time and frustration.

We're embroiderers ourselves. We know the pain of getting a PES file when your machine needs DST. We know the struggle of finding Anchor equivalents when the store only stocks DMC. These tools were born from our own stitching headaches.

🪡 What We Offer

Embroidery File Converter: Upload any embroidery file (47+ input formats) and download it converted to 19 output formats — DST, PES, JEF, EXP, VP3, XXX, and more. All in one ZIP. Files auto-deleted after 10 minutes for your privacy.

Embroidery Floss Matcher: Search across 456 DMC colors with hex codes, and instantly find equivalents in Anchor, Cosmo, and J&P Coats. 449 Cosmo matches, 107 Anchor equivalents.

Blog: 68 free embroidery guides and tutorials — written by stitchers, for stitchers. File format guides, thread comparisons, technique tutorials, and more.

🔒 Privacy & Trust

We don't require accounts. We don't store your files. We don't track you. Uploaded embroidery files are automatically deleted after 10 minutes. Our tools are free because we believe embroidery resources should be accessible to everyone — from hobbyists to professional digitizers.
