How to Hoop Fabric for Embroidery: Perfect Alignment Every Time

Published June 04, 2025 — EmbroideryConvert Team

Bad hooping is the #1 cause of misaligned embroidery. Here's how to hoop like a pro — no more crooked designs or shifting fabric.

📎 Embroidery Hoop Size Guide

The Golden Rules of Hooping

  1. Hoop stabilizer WITH fabric — never hoop fabric alone
  2. Drum-tight tension — fabric should sound like a drum when tapped
  3. Grain-aligned — fabric threads should be perpendicular to hoop edges
  4. Don't over-tighten — the screw should be snug, not wrenched

Step-by-Step Hooping

  1. Place outer hoop on a flat surface
  2. Lay stabilizer on top
  3. Lay fabric on top, aligned to grain
  4. Press inner hoop down — don't slide, press straight
  5. Tighten screw
  6. Check tension — tap the center, it should sound like a drum
  7. If loose, tighten screw slightly more

Float Method (Delicate Fabrics)

For velvet, silk, leather, or items that can't be hooped: hoop only the stabilizer, then use temporary spray adhesive to stick the fabric on top. The needle passes through fabric + stabilizer without the fabric being crushed by the hoop.
