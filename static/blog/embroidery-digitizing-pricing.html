Embroidery Digitizing Pricing: How Much to Charge for Custom Designs

Home › Blog › This Article
Published May 19, 2025 — EmbroideryConvert Team

Digitizing is skilled work. Here's how to price it so you're not working for minimum wage.

📎 Embroidery Digitizing Vs Converting📎 Embroidery Pricing Guide

Industry Rates (2025)

  • Simple text/logo (3,000-5,000 stitches): $10-25
  • Left chest logo (5,000-8,000 stitches): $15-40
  • Complex logo (8,000-15,000 stitches): $30-75
  • Jacket back (15,000-30,000 stitches): $50-150
  • Photo-realistic (30,000+ stitches): $100-300+

Pricing Models

  • Per 1,000 stitches: $2-7 per thousand. Good for complex work.
  • Flat rate by complexity: Simple ($15), Medium ($35), Complex ($75), Photo ($150+). Good for quoting.
  • Hourly: $25-75/hour. Pro digitizers digitize 3,000-8,000 stitches per hour. Charge for your time, not the machine's.
