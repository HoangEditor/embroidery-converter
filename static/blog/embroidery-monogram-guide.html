Embroidery Monogram Guide: Styles, Placement & File Formats

Home › Blog › This Article
Published June 11, 2025 — EmbroideryConvert Team

Monograms are timeless. Here's everything about embroidery monogram styles, placement, and how to get the files right.

Monogram Styles

  • Traditional (Victorian): First initial, LAST initial (larger, center), Middle initial. Example: Mary Jane Smith = mSj
  • Modern (First-Last): First, Middle, Last — all same size. Example: MJS
  • Single Initial: Last name initial only. Classic for handkerchiefs and shirt cuffs.

Placement Guide

  • Shirt cuff: 1/4 inch above cuff edge, centered
  • Towel: 2 inches above hem, centered (or 4 inches for bath sheets)
  • Pillowcase: Centered on the cuff, 1 inch from open edge
  • Napkin: Bottom left or right corner, angled or centered

File Format Tips

Monogram fonts are often sold as individual letter files. You'll need to combine them in digitizing software, or use a monogramming program. Output in your machine's format — convert if needed.
