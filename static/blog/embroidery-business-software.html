Best Software for Embroidery Business: Digitizing, Accounting & Workflow

Published May 15, 2025 — EmbroideryConvert Team

Running an embroidery business means more than just stitching. Here's the software stack that keeps professional shops organized.

📎 Embroidery Digitizing Vs Converting📎 Embroidery Digitizing Pricing

1. Digitizing Software

  • Wilcom EmbroideryStudio: Industry standard. $2,500+. Full digitizing, editing, lettering.
  • Hatch by Wilcom: Consumer version. $800-1,200. 80% of the power at 30% of the cost.
  • Embrilliance: Modular pricing. Start with Essentials ($150), add features as needed.
  • Ink/Stitch: Free, open-source. Steep learning curve but capable.

2. Order Management

  • ShopWorks: Designed for embroidery/decorating shops. Billing, inventory, scheduling. $150+/month.
  • Printavo: More affordable shop management. $150/month. Good for smaller shops.

3. Design Organization

  • Embrilliance: Built-in design catalog.
  • SophieSew: Free design organizer.
  • Dropbox/Google Drive: Cloud backup and sharing with clients.

4. File Conversion

Every shop needs a reliable converter. https://embroidery-file-converter.onrender.com handles 47+ input formats → 19 output formats. Free, no signup. Essential for receiving client files in any format and outputting to your production machines.
