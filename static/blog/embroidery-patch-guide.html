How to Make Custom Embroidery Patches: Complete DIY Guide

Home › Blog › This Article
Published June 13, 2025 — EmbroideryConvert Team

Custom patches are a huge market — biker clubs, sports teams, brands, and fashion. Here's how to make them.

Patch-Making Process

  1. Design & Digitize: Create your artwork, digitize for embroidery. Add a border stitch for cutting.
  2. Stitch on twill or felt: Hoop patch material (poly twill is standard) with heavy cut-away stabilizer.
  3. Stitch the design: Run the embroidery. Convert to your machine's format — DST for commercial, PES for Brother, etc.
  4. Cut out: Use scissors or a hot knife. Follow the border stitch line.
  5. Edge finishing (optional): Merrow border (overlock stitch around the edge) for a classic patch look. Requires a merrowing machine or attachment.
  6. Apply backing: Iron-on adhesive, sew-on, or Velcro — depending on customer needs.

File Format Tips

Patches are typically digitized as solid fill designs with a satin border. The file format doesn't affect the patch quality — DST, PES, JEF all stitch identically for patches.
