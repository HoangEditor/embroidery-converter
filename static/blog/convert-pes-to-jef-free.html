How to Convert PES to JEF — Free Online (2025 Guide)

Published 2025 — EmbroideryConvert Team

Got a Brother PES design but you own a Janome machine? Janome uses JEF format, not PES. Here's the quickest way to convert.

Quick Answer

Upload your PES file to EmbroideryConvert, select JEF as output, and download — free, no signup.

Why PES and JEF Are Different

Both store colors and stitch data, but they use different color encoding. Brother PES uses Brother's palette; JEF uses Janome's. When converting, our tool maps colors to the closest Janome equivalent. The stitches transfer exactly.

Step-by-Step PES to JEF Conversion

  1. Go to https://embroidery-file-converter.onrender.com
  2. Upload your PES file (drag & drop or browse)
  3. Check JEF in the format list
  4. Click Convert
  5. Download the ZIP — your JEF file is inside

Will My Colors Look Right?

Mostly yes. Brother and Janome have similar color palettes. Dark blue in PES maps to dark blue in JEF — the shade may differ slightly but it's usually close enough. For precision color matching, check with our floss color matcher.

Batch Converting Multiple Files

Need to convert a whole folder? Convert one at a time — each takes 5-15 seconds. For very large libraries (100+ files), consider a desktop batch converter.
