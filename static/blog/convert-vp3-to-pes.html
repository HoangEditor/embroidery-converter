Convert VP3 to PES: Pfaff/Husqvarna to Brother Format Free

Published 2025 — EmbroideryConvert Team

Pfaff and Husqvarna Viking machines use VP3 format. If you're switching to a Brother or need to share designs with a Brother user, you need to convert VP3 to PES.

VP3 Format Basics

VP3 stores stitches with color information and machine settings (tension, speed). Converting to PES preserves stitches and maps colors to Brother's palette.

How to Convert VP3 to PES

  1. Upload your VP3 file at https://embroidery-file-converter.onrender.com
  2. Select PES as output format
  3. Click Convert and download the ZIP

What to Expect

  • Stitches: 100% preserved
  • Colors: Mapped to Brother palette (close matches)
  • Hoop size: Preserved in PES metadata
  • Machine settings: Lost (PES doesn't store VP3-specific settings)
