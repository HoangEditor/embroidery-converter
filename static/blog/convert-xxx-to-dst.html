Convert XXX to DST: Singer to Universal Embroidery Format

Published 2025 — EmbroideryConvert Team

Singer embroidery machines use XXX format. If you need to run a Singer design on a commercial Tajima or Barudan, you need DST. Here's the free conversion method.

📎 Convert U01 To Dst

Singer XXX Format

XXX is Singer's native embroidery format, used on Futura and Quantum machines. It stores stitches with basic color information. DST is stitch-only — colors are lost during conversion but stitches are 100% preserved.

Singer Machine Compatibility
