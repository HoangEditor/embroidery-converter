Getting Started

Before diving into top 20 surface embroidery stitches, make sure you have the right foundation. Quality materials make a huge difference.

Step-by-Step

  1. Prepare your fabric with the appropriate stabilizer
  2. Thread your needle with quality embroidery floss
  3. Start with a waste knot
  4. Work your stitches consistently and evenly
  5. Secure threads neatly on the back
  6. Press your finished work carefully

Common Mistakes

  • Pulling thread too tight — this causes puckering
  • Using the wrong needle size
  • Skipping stabilizer on stretchy fabrics
  • Not securing thread ends properly
