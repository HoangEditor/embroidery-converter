Embroidery Thread Weight Conversion Chart: wt, denier, tex Explained

Home › Blog › This Article
Published July 02, 2025 — EmbroideryConvert Team

Thread weight labeling is confusing — wt, denier, tex, ticket number. Here's the cheat sheet.

The Systems

  • Weight (wt): Higher number = thinner thread. 40wt is standard machine embroidery. Common in US.
  • Denier: Grams per 9,000 meters. Higher = thicker. Common for poly/rayon.
  • Tex: Grams per 1,000 meters. Higher = thicker. Common in Europe/industrial.
  • Ticket (Nm): Common in Europe for cotton threads.

Quick Conversion
