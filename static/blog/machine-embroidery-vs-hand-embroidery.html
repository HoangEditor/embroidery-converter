Machine Embroidery vs Hand Embroidery: Which Should You Choose?

Published July 16, 2025 — EmbroideryConvert Team

Should you buy an embroidery machine or stick to hand embroidery? Both have their place. Here's the honest comparison.

Machine Embroidery

Pros

  • Speed: 400-1,200 stitches per minute — a 10,000-stitch design finishes in 10-25 minutes
  • Consistency: Identical results every time — great for production runs
  • Complexity: Photo-realistic designs, tiny text, 15+ colors in one design
  • Durability: Dense stitching for caps, bags, uniforms — it lasts

Cons

  • Cost: $300-$8,000 for the machine + ongoing supplies
  • Learning curve: File formats, digitizing, machine maintenance
  • Noise: Machines are loud
  • Less personal: It's software-driven, not hand-guided

Hand Embroidery

Pros

  • Cost: $20-100 for hoops, needles, floss — incredibly affordable
  • Portable: Stitch anywhere — couch, park, coffee shop
  • Artistic control: Every stitch is placed by your hand — unique results
  • Therapeutic: Slow, meditative, proven to reduce stress

Cons

  • Speed: A 6-inch design takes 20-40 hours by hand
  • Inconsistency: Each piece is slightly different (good for art, bad for business)
  • Repetitive strain: Hands, wrists, eyes get tired
