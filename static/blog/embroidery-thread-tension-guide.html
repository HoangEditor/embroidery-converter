Embroidery Thread Tension Guide: Fix Loops, Birds Nests & Breakage

Published 2025 — EmbroideryConvert Team

Thread tension is the #1 cause of embroidery problems. Too tight and the fabric puckers. Too loose and you get loops. Here's the complete tension guide.

Understanding Top vs Bottom Tension

Embroidery machines use two tension systems: top thread (through the needle) and bobbin thread (underneath). They need to balance — the lock stitch should meet in the middle of the fabric.

Symptoms of Bad Tension

  • Top thread looping underneath: Top tension too loose or bobbin too tight
  • Bobbin thread showing on top: Top tension too tight or bobbin too loose
  • Puckering fabric: Both tensions too tight
  • Thread breakage: Top tension too tight or thread path obstructed
  • Bird's nest underneath: Top thread not seated in tension discs

Digital Designs Affect Tension Too

The embroidery file itself influences tension. Overly dense designs force the needle through tight spots, increasing breakage. If a specific design always causes problems, check its density in software. Our converter preserves stitch data as-is — edit density before converting.

Thread Quality Matters

Cheap thread breaks more. Use quality brands like Madeira, Isacord, or Robison-Anton for machine embroidery. For hand embroidery, DMC and Anchor are reliable. Match thread colors across brands.
