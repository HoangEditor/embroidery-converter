Why DST to PES conversion matters

DST is the industry standard for Tajima and Barudan commercial machines. PES is the format for Brother, Babylock, and home embroidery machines. If you're a digitizer sending files to clients with different machines, you need both. Doing it manually in embroidery software is slow and repetitive.

DST vs PES: what's the difference?

DST stores stitch coordinates as relative movements — it's compact and universal. PES adds color change commands and thread trimming instructions that home machines need. When you convert DST to PES, the tool preserves all stitch data while adding the metadata PES requires.
